Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?+

No. Text is processed in your browser and is not uploaded. The free quota stores only a date and use count in localStorage.

What can I clean for free?+

You get 3 cleanings per day on this device, up to 500 words each, with no sign-up. Scanning and reviewing do not use the allowance.

Does the cleaner rewrite my draft or remove statistical watermarks?+

No. It cleans selected formatting and character artifacts and flags writing patterns for your review. It does not paraphrase, verify authorship, or remove statistical vendor watermarks.

How do vendor watermarks and Unicode artifacts differ?+

Among the vendor systems covered here, Microsoft Windows AI text APIs are the character-based exception: Microsoft documents the sequence U+200B U+034F U+2060 U+200B. OpenAI textGrain, Claude watermarking, SynthID Text and Grok API watermarking use statistical signals. Statistical watermarks encode signals through word choice, not hidden characters. Heavy rewriting can disrupt those signals, but character cleaning cannot; this app does not perform heavy rewriting.
