NoAITell Privacy Policy

Learn how NoAITell processes text locally and stores the free quota counter on your device.

Effective November 4, 2026. Text you paste into the cleaner is processed in your browser. NoAITell does not upload it, save it to an account, or use it for training. This release has no accounts or cloud rewriting. Monthly checkout is available in test mode through Waffo Pancake.

The free quota stores a date and cleaning count under noaitell:quota:v1 in localStorage. It does not store your draft. The counter is specific to this browser profile and origin. Clearing site data deletes it. If storage is blocked, cleaning cannot record a use and is unavailable; inspection still works.

Input and results remain in page memory while you work. Copying a result uses your device clipboard. Reloading closes that in-memory session. Browser extensions or operating system clipboard services follow their own settings.

Cloudflare Pages serves the site and may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and user agents according to its hosting policies. External source links take you to sites with separate privacy policies. No source link receives the text you pasted.

Waffo Pancake hosts test checkout and collects the email and test payment details you enter there. Use test details only. Signed payment notifications store your email, order ID, plan, status, billing period and delivery ID in Cloudflare D1 for test entitlements and duplicate prevention. Draft text and card details are not stored in D1. Contact us to request deletion of test payment records. For a privacy issue, email noaitell@knowhy.net and omit personal or sensitive draft text.

When configured by the site operator, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity collect site usage information and may use cookies. The cleaner editor is excluded from Clarity recording. Draft text is not sent to analytics. The Pro notification form opens your email app; sending it shares your email address with us so we can reply about launch availability.

Contact: noaitell@knowhy.net.
