NoAITell Refund Policy

NoAITell does not currently accept payments. Read the status of upcoming paid plans and refunds.

Effective November 4, 2026. NoAITell currently offers a free local cleaner and does not accept payments. Pro launch notifications are available by email. Monthly subscriptions can be simulated in Waffo test checkout; no live charges are collected. Test entitlements do not unlock the planned Pro features.

The pricing page previews a $4.99 24-hour day pass, a $19.9 monthly plan and a $199 yearly plan. These prices do not create an order. Waffo Pancake test checkout and signed webhooks are connected for Monthly. Day Pass and Yearly checkout remain unavailable. For test cancellation or refund help, email noaitell@knowhy.net with your test order ID; do not send card details.

Before paid plans launch, this page will provide the applicable refund request process, support contact, eligibility conditions and cancellation information. Those details will be available before checkout, together with the final purchase terms.

If you see a charge you believe is associated with NoAITell, check the merchant information on your receipt and contact the payment provider shown there. Do not send card details or private drafts through a public issue tracker. Any mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Contact: noaitell@knowhy.net.
